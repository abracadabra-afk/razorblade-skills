# EP 90 - FIXTURE RADIO · spine

**Premise:** A commuting man turns on the radio in his car. Between stations, in the static, he hears his daughter's voice.
**Promise:** She's been dead for two years.
**Shape:** slow burn

## Step 2 — Want vs Inescapability

### Want
- first physical move — Drive home. Get through the commute. Don't think about the heavy stuff.

### Rationale
- Much easier to ignore than facing the haunting truth of what happened his daughter.

### Inescapable
- force — His daughter wants to be heard. No matter what he tries to stop it.
- truth — What happened to her, and his part in it.

### Story
- must — silence her accusations
- or else — admit the horrible truth of what happened to her

### Setting
- the static has a rhythm, almost like breathing · force
- the temperature in the car is dropping with the heater on · force
- her voice is exactly too quiet to hear unless you are listening for it · truth
- placing facts — none needed

## Step 3 — Escalations

### Opening (~350)
- the witnessed instance on the page, the want in the first move

### E1 (~450)
- trigger — He hears his daughter's voice on the radio.
- therefore — The man turns off the radio.
- but — The radio turns back on. · denies: that the radio is a thing he controls
- penalty — Forces him to think about his dead daughter. · subtracts: the commute as a place where he does not think about her

### E2 (~500)
- trigger — The radio won't stay off.
- therefore — The man changes the station to something else.
- but — His daughter's voice bleeds through the broadcast on every channel. · denies: that there is a channel without her on it
- penalty — Forces him to think of her accusations about him. · subtracts: the version of her that was only a voice, not an accuser

### E3 (~550)
- trigger — Nothing stops her voice or his thoughts about her.
- therefore — He pulls over on the side of the road and shuts the car off. Silence at last.
- but — The radio turns back on, speaking to him directly, asking him to admit his guilt. · denies: that cutting the power cuts her off
- penalty — Must face his apex fear, confess what he had never wanted to. · subtracts: the last place the accusation was not aimed at him by name

### Climax (~500)
- ultimate therefore — He pops the hood, gets out of the car, and tears the battery out. Throws it onto the street, refuses providing any further energy to the source of his agony.

### Cut (~100)
- last line on the page — Silence achieved, car wrecked, deeper wound averted, until he hears his daughter's voice right behind him. Not buried in the static.

### Pay-off (~0)
- survives off the page — Cannot escape his daughter, or his sins, by trying to snuff them out in silence. They are literally behind him now. The pursuit continues off page.
