# The checks — each with its reason, its verdict, and what it does not see

Every check reports **pass** or **FLAG**. A flag is never a veto: CRE's gut outranks the check, and the check exists to catch a dread-less premise before a word is dictated. Structural problems (a missing section, a budget that does not sum, an unparsable frontmatter) are **FAIL** from `engine.py` and are the tool's to fix, never CRE's.

## The promise mapping (dec-036 carried as the mapping only)

Five lines, read twice — at Step 1 against the two sentences, and again before Step 3 is stamped against the filled slots:

| Line | Reads from | At Step 1 | Before the stamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| **dread** — something bad is going to happen | the promise sentence | is there an approaching harm the reader feels coming? | unchanged — still the promise |
| **fresh wound** — witnessed, on the page, story-present | the premise sentence | is it one instance, not a scar? | unchanged |
| **destruction** — something is destroyed | the promise, best pre-read | what will be lost? | now reads from **Story · or else** |
| **compelled to watch** — the reader stays in the room | the premise, best pre-read | why can't the reader look away? | now reads from **Want + Rationale** |
| **no agency** — the character cannot get what they want now | the promise, best pre-read | what refuses them? | now reads from **Inescapable · force + truth** |

Dread reads first and is the one that kills fastest; a premise with no dread source is cruelty in an unbent world, which clears every other line and is not horror. A thin line is recorded `thin — [NOT NAMED — CRE]` on the triage's `## Promise read`; the candidate still banks (Substack paid / anthology / submission are not gated), the flag rides its row, and `episode-init` re-rules at the gate. **Dropped from dec-036 for short form:** its "flaw discovered in the response" clause — there is no flaw in this spine.

## Inescapable names both halves

Force alone is a mechanism (a radio that will not turn off). Truth alone is literary (a father's guilt). The horror is a force that *is* the truth wearing a shape. The check: both lines filled; not the same words restated. It does not see whether the truth is the right one.

## Each Penalty subtracts something new

The only mechanical proof of escalation. A list of incidents can have three penalties that all say "she is more afraid." The check compares the `subtracts:` clauses for restatement (same content words, or three-quarters overlap) and flags a repeat. It does not see whether a loss is real.

## Setting is 3–4 details, each tied

Texture is filled at the mic (DIR-017 §2); the plan carries only the early signatures of the Inescapable. Fewer than three and the setting is not doing the Inescapable's work; more than four and the plan is pre-spending the mic. Each line ends `· force` or `· truth`. Era, region, class appear only as `placing facts —` and only when the premise needs them (`placing_facts_needed: true` in frontmatter, his reason in the line). The placing-facts line is `[NOT NAMED — CRE]` until he answers — "none needed" is his answer, never a scaffold default, and a `[NOT NAMED — CRE]` there is counted as a hole, not read as a named fact. A detail carrying an era or region word is flagged.

## Budget derivation (when he gives no numbers)

`engine.py budget` — fixed template over the band midpoint: Opening 14% · escalations 62% rising by 50 per beat · Climax 20% · Cut 4% · Pay-off 0; rounded to 50; remainder on Climax. Deterministic by construction so two runs on one spine do not disagree. Presented for one-tap ratify; his numbers replace it whole.

## The stamp line

A draft spine with holes carries `stamp — draft · holes: …` on `## Checks`, every hole by slot. `check-spine` fails a holed draft without the line and flags a hole the line does not name. It is the deferral record the next sitting reads, so nothing routes through CRE's memory.

## Want is a first physical move

"wants to" / "hopes to" in the want line is a declaration, flagged. The line should read as a thing the character does — turning off the radio, stacking the chair.

## Budget sums inside the band

Opening + each E + Climax + Cut, with Pay-off at `(~0)` because it is not written. Verdict IN-BAND / UNDER-BAND / OVER-BAND against `band_low`/`band_high` (2,000–2,800 from strategy §3b, passed as arguments so the tool carries no numbers of its own). Out of band is a flag: the band is prescriptive at plan time and CRE rules reshape or route-out; the draft is never graded against the plan (DIR-017).

## What none of the checks see (DIR-018)

Whether a slot is CRE's words or an invented beat. Whether a Therefore is really forced by its But. Whether the dread is dread. Whether a penalty is a real loss. Whether the premise sparks. Those are his gut, and impact past the checks is not scored.
