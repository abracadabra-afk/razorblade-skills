# branch-writing-expert

**What it does.** The single front door to Branch Writing, CRE's Obsidian plugin for plotting and writing his novels. He
feeds it information at any level — structure, cast, eras, relationships, systems, items, tags, a dump of story ideas — and
it executes on the board he names or is working in, live boards included. It executes his ideas, never changes them, never
writes prose, and shows a short change list after each run. Every write is logged per board so it can undo and redo its
own work byte-for-byte. It reads the board fresh every run and checks the installed plugin and the schema against its
calibration record at every launch (CRE rulings (a)–(h), 2026-10-18; the intent).

**Modes.**

| CRE says | Mode | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| "work the board" / "board: …" / any instruction about a board | Work the board | direct instructions execute (cast, eras, relationships, world entries, tags, links, containers, fields, plotting switch, leads, exits); inferences are a ruling list he approves first |
| "here is a dump of my story ideas" → "match them to beats" | Idea dump → placement | ideas filed verbatim in the board's inbox, typed; one placement proposal per idea with its reason; approved ideas move to their card's tray, the rest stay in the inbox |
| "import to the board" | Import (prose → board) | the plugin's convert rule, word for word, into a new board (a short story note, or one board per book from `CHAPTERS/*/draft.md`, marked a working copy) — or, with `--into`, onto a board he already built, as one undoable run that leaves every existing card byte-identical (2026-10-19); scenes, chapters, dialogue speakers, cast and POV proposed and ruled; sources never changed |
| "break down chapter N" / "break down the book" | Breakdown (the administrative levels) | his paragraphs split into one typed micro-beat per beat (words exact, proven by hash), Beats grouped with title and summary (never a beat type: since plugin 0.70 a Beat's badge is read live from its scene line), each Scene and Sequence titled, summarized and chained, scenes linked to the chapter's lines and beats to their scene's (one scene line per beat); chains stop at the Scene unless he gives a beat its own; pilot chapter first; written directly, logged, undoable (`references/breakdown.md`) |
| "interlock the arcs" · "build the interlock board" · "check the interlock" | Plotting (the former `interlock-plotter`) | plan in his words → his approval → a new board or a copy via the plugin's node builder → the story-bible setup → the check report, observations only |
| "tag the board" | Tagging (the former "AI tagger") | tags and links as `by: ai` entries; beat types, speakers, summaries, wants, states and a chain summary under the `ai` marker key; intensity to the board's sidecar; route A (rule first) or route B (write as offers) |
| after a build, or "set up the bible" | Story-bible setup | a placeholder era when none is named, every unplaced step filed in it; roles and the relationships the model's roles state, proposed and ruled; nothing invented |
| "audit the board" | Audit (read-only) | the whole board crawled into story-order digests (`audit_crawl.mjs`), then five checks — continuity, beat mapping, alignment with the plan, meaning issues around plotting, repetition — each candidate verified (exact quote, every POV strand searched, his markers and rulings respected); a report in `SYSTEM/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-<board>-audit.md` ranked by how much each finding would strain a reader, story problems as questions for him, housekeeping with its mechanical fix. Never writes the board. Offered once after a part lands on a live board (`references/audit.md`; intent `WORKFLOWS/intents/branch-writing-board-audit.md`) |
| "trace X" / "trace the X and the Y" | Trace (read-only) | one thread — a character, object, relationship, force or piece of lore — followed through every source family on the project's source map (`WRITING/PROJECTS/<PROJECT>/trace-sources.md`; built from the tree and confirmed by him once when a project has none), one read-only reader per family in parallel (about 6), every quote exact with file and line and labelled his or a file's, every POV strand searched before anything is called missing, each call resolved against his rulings and the newer drafts first. A report in `SYSTEM/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-<project>-trace-<thread>.md`: throughline, story-order table, stable core, disagreements ranked by whether they touch the current book, gaps against the grid, his calls then "resolved — confirm", stale notes for canon-sync, a subplot step table (plain step lines carrying his exact words, labelled redraft markers for the rest), what wasn't checked. Never writes the board, plan or DEV; the step table goes on only on his "send it", as a separate run through the plan's subplots and `subplot_build.mjs` (`references/trace.md`; intent `WORKFLOWS/intents/branch-writing-trace-thread.md`) |
| "finish the bible" / "walk the world" | World walk | a built board's side panels filled from his own sources (the board's words, `DEV/registry/`, `REFERENCE/threads.md`, the approved plan) in eight stages, one approval list each, the next only after he rules: registry entries and cast → scene links → the era walk → seeds and payoffs from the thread list (span rule) → tagging mode → event pins → profiles → idea admin. Story inferences are proposals; links that follow an approved entry, idea types, `by: ai` tags and byte-identical twins are admin, written directly. Side-panel text is plain words drawn from his, with his lines kept in `notes`. Words proof (micro-beats and ideas) before and after every write; a report in `SYSTEM/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-<board>-world-walk.md` (`references/world-walk.md`, `scripts/world_walk.mjs`; intent `WORKFLOWS/intents/branch-writing-world-walk.md`) |
| "stage the beats for <scene>" / "stage scene X" / "brief the beats" / "restage <scene>" | Stage the beats | one finalized scene's map laid out as a beat brief, so he writes without holding it: a scene header, then per beat what happens, what it accomplishes (each arc step on the beat's line, its arc, goal and the step before it), coming in (the previous beat, the previous beat in the same timeline, the plants it relies on), states from → to, and a checklist (Land · Plant · Pay off · Foreshadow · Move · Show · Weight · Use · Open) with every line sourced; a beat he bundled over several lines is one brief with exchange rows. Steps sort onto beats mechanically (`plotLine` / `plotFrom`, runs included); the scene's ideas go onto beats by his ruling list. The scene is stamped staged (date, structure fingerprint, path) and linked to the brief; a changed scene reads stale and is rebuilt on his word. Never prose, never a chain line, never a summary (`references/stage.md`, `scripts/stage_scene.mjs`, `scripts/stage_front.py`; intent [[WORKFLOWS/intents/branch-writing-stage-the-beats]]) |
| "undo the last run" / "redo" | Undo / redo | the touched cards restored or re-applied byte-for-byte from the board's change log; a hand-edited card is named and left alone |

**Where things land.**
- The board he names (live or new). Change logs: `SYSTEM/board-logs/<board>.md` (his default; out of his sight — the chat change list is what he sees).
- Beat briefs: `WRITING/BOARD BRIEFS/<board>/Ch NN - <chapter title>.md` (the scene title when a chapter has several scenes), linked from the scene.
- Plans: `WRITING/BOARD PLANS/<board> - arc plan.md`; check reports: `SYSTEM/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-<board>-interlock-check.md`; intensity offers: `<board>.measure.json` beside the board (schema §6a).
- Calibration record: `WORKFLOWS/skills-src/branch-writing-expert/calibration.json` (the plugin version, the schema doc's version and hash, the node core's hash it was calibrated to). The tool reads the vault copy first, so a recalibration lands in the source and ships at the next pack.
- Test runs: `SYSTEM/skill-tests/branch-writing/_work-*`.

**Tools** (in the skill's `scripts/`; host scripts run from the desktop shell, Git Bash — every JSON input is a file, never a shell argument, `^obs-444`).
- `board_tool.mjs` — the one writer, over the plugin's own pure core (`C:\Users\Chad\dev\branch-writing\test\core.mjs`, built by `npm run build`): `check` (version check) · `read` · `apply <ops.json> [--dry]` · `undo` · `redo` · `import` · `propose` · `suggest-tags` · `log` · `calibrate` · `normalize`. Ops shapes: `references/ops.md`. It refuses to write when the version check is not `ok`, when the board doesn't round-trip through the plugin's serializer, when a field is the author's (summary, statements, outcomes, chain, marks, Complete), and when a value would reword or invent. `add-event` (2026-10-18) writes an event of an existing type on his word ("Wren is born in 1985"), checked in full before anything is written.
- `split_plan.mjs`, `admin_plan.mjs`, `words.mjs` (2026-10-19) — the breakdown's planners (split spec → `split-micro` ops; chapter plan → regroup / titles / summaries / chains / links, refused unless every micro-beat is covered once in order) and the words proof (one hash of every micro-beat's words in order, before and after each run).
- `audit_crawl.mjs` (2026-10-21) — audit mode's crawler, read-only: board (+ plan) → a scratch folder of digests in story order (outline and prose per part, arcs with inversions and silences, markers by class, plan-vs-board alignment, name silences, repeats) and a README of what the crawl did not check. Under a second on the 6,647-card Interlock board.
- `plan_tool.py` — the plotting plan (carried over from interlock-plotter, plus `set --json-file`; 2026-10-19: extra spine sets, `spines` + `--set <key>`).
- `grade.mjs` — the proof grader: every string given is on the board and nothing else, untouched cards byte-identical, undo/redo byte-exact, and the mode-specific criteria.
- Plugin dev folder: `tools/build-plan.mjs` (new board or copy from an approved plan), `tools/check-board.mjs`.

**Guardrails, with reasons** (full statements in the intent).
- *Execute his instructions; propose his inferences* — a suggestion on the board anchors him (CDIR-003); an executed guess is his to find and undo.
- *His words, verbatim; never prose* — the organic process breaks the moment the tool writes story (CDIR-001). Open slots stay open, labelled blanks.
- *Live boards under the log* — the log entry is written before the board; a run that cannot log doesn't write. Undo restores byte-for-byte; a card he edited since is left alone and named.
- *Touch only what you change* — the plugin's parse/serialize round trip is the gate; keys hidden never deleted; unknown keys preserved.
- *Version check first, automatic* — a tool working from a stale picture of the format is the one way it can damage a board; a check he has to remember doesn't happen. `newer` → read the build report's later sections and the schema's changed sections, then `calibrate`; a change it can't account for → read-only, said plainly.
- *Tagging reads ruled terms, writes none* — vocabulary from `REFERENCE/_LEXICON.md` and `_DEV_MAP` read-only (DIR-014); a `by: cre` entry is never touched; dev-edit's blind seats never see the network.
- *Story-bible setup never invents* — a placeholder era (BW U13), roles from the model role, relationships only where roles state them; no Place, Item or System the plan doesn't hold.
- *Ensembles, 1..N leads* — proven at one lead and at five.
- *No clicks in his window; attended only.*

**Lead exits on a Sequence (fixed in plugin 0.62.2, 2026-10-19).** The plugin now reads an exit stored on a Sequence (or Part, Book) at the last scene inside it (`exitUnitId`), so the builder's exit on whatever container holds the exit step shows as "exits at …" in the Plot tab. A container with no scene yet reads "stays to the end" until one is added, and both the builder and `set-lead-exit` say so as a NOT DONE line. The earlier workaround (refuse a Sequence; place exit steps on a Scene) is gone; `set-lead-exit` keeps the refusal only against an older core without `exitUnitId`. Closed `^backlog-bw-lead-exit-unit`.

**A second full spine set (plugin 0.65, CRE 2026-10-19: "extend the tool so the Woodsgirl arc is its own full spine").** One character can lead two stories on one board — the woodswoman's present day and, in the interludes, the Woodsgirl's childhood in full, mirroring it, with Death as the antagonist of both. A plan's `spines` holds each extra set (key, name, lead, antagonist, era, its own K1–K6, its own beats and walked rounds); `plan_tool.py` walks it with `--set <key>` and a plan without `spines` reads and renders exactly as before, so both the old and new tool read either file. The builder makes the set's lead, antagonist and external arcs on the plan's cast (reused, never duplicated), named "<template> · <set name>", with one external plot Arc per set and only the main set's marked main; the checks, the check report and the Visuals Interlock grid run within each set (a lead in each set is no clash). On a copy, arcs a character already has are never reassigned to a set — which arc carries which spine is his call, asked as a NOT DONE line. Schema §5r; `references/plotting.md` step 4b. The 0.64.2 builder fix rides with it: a copy reuses the cast already on the board (exact name or nickname), fills only empty profile fields, and holds a second arc in a role a character already has for his pick.

**The administrative levels (CRE-ruled 2026-10-19).** "The prose level (micro-beats) are my words — those are hands off. But the levels above it are administrative." Above the micro-beats, the expert reads the prose and writes, logged and undoable, without a ruling list first: Beat grouping (runs of micro-beats, in order, every one covered), Beat titles and summaries — **never a Beat type** (CRE 2026-10-16, built 2026-10-23 in plugin 0.70: a Beat's position — setup, escalation, outcome — is read live from the scene line it is linked to, and climax / valley / hook are his marks on that line); a micro-beat's type (never `dialogue` on a card that doesn't open with a quote); a Scene's title, summary, goal holder and chain, and a Sequence's title, summary and chain (ENTRY → BUT / THEREFORE → EXIT, E3 outcomes) — **from the sequence up, goal holders are read live from the scenes beneath** (CRE 2026-10-23, plugin 0.71.2: "there maybe several goal holders in a part, a sequence, a book"); his own list there (`goal holders`) is his alone — carried up from the beats. Ops: `regroup-beats`, `set-title`, `set-summary`, `set-chain` (`references/ops.md`); plan → ops with `scripts/admin_plan.mjs`. Pilot one chapter, let him read it on the board, then run the rest. Still his: the micro-beats' words and order, POV, eras, Complete marks, and any call he says is his. **Splitting micro-beats (CRE, 2026-10-19):** the board is his plotting and planning tool — "separation and identification of the chains is what i built it for so I can identify what is missing, write the draft text for each piece, and export those pieces into a working draft that I can then format and modify as I like." So a paragraph that fires several micro-beats is split into one card per micro-beat, each typed, at the sentence where the type changes: grouping and identification are admin, **the words themselves are the forbidden zone** — every piece keeps its words exactly, in order, nothing added or dropped (an italic run split across pieces is re-wrapped per piece so it still reads italic). Paragraphing on export is not protected: the export is his working draft, which he formats. **Depth (CRE, 2026-10-19):** chains stop at the Scene. A Beat carries its title and summary — the contract he fills — and its micro-beats under it, typed, with no beat chain and no ENTRY / BUT / THEREFORE slots ("beat to microbeat is a drill too far").

**Not for:** prose, premises, character ideas, rewording an idea; export / Copy clean (a plugin click); the pipeline files, `REFERENCE/`, StoryLine's Codex; editing the model or Statement Plotting text (CDIR-002); migrating boards between schema versions; deciding which copy of imported prose is canon (`^backlog-branch-writing-replacement-review`); scheduled runs.
