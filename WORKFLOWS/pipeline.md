# The pipeline (how the fiction workflows chain)

> Carved verbatim from [[_SKILLS MAP]] on 2026-07-10 (the boot-cost slim, [[SYSTEM/reports/2026-07-10-os-audit-improvements]]). The pre-slim map is archived at `SYSTEM/history/_SKILLS MAP-2026-07-10-pre-slim.md`. This doc is the single home for the pipeline-relationship canon; the map holds only the trigger index.

## Pipeline relationship

dictation-preflight is the upstream prep (queued dictation → filled `envelope.md`, authoring the Transcoder's required input — the `^obs-005` fix). Transcoder is next (raw dictation + envelope → clean rough-draft slate, **generative**). spec-check is an **optional, selective diagnostic tier** between the slate and the register: Pass 1 (`blind-read` skill) runs cold in a clean room; the **developmental pass** (`blind-response` skill) then optionally revises the chapter from the blind read into `draft.md` (two-phase gated — CRE rules the triage and approves the fixes; Pass-1 findings + structure only, never line/voice); Passes 2–5 are spec-aware and run on the **working text** (`draft.md` if `status: dev-revised`, else the slate clean-draft) — either via the `spec-passes` runner (one isolated subagent per pass, recommended) or pasted by hand — and CRE reconciles the bins into `spec-check/<slate-run>/verdicts.md` — by hand or via the `reconcile` skill, which walks the judgment calls one at a time, enforces the first-UNDRAMATIZED hand-check, and finalizes the sheet to `status: ready` for `register-pass`. register-pass is the revision/execution stage out of the slate (newest slate `clean-draft.md` → revised passage + editorial note in `revisions/`, running the **project-specific** register at `REFERENCE/register.md` — the dedicated workflow the chapter `revisions/README` files previously called "TBD"). register-pass is **coupled to spec-check use-if-present**: when a ready `verdicts.md` exists for the slate run it runs *execute-only* (honors CRE's settled rulings, builds UNDRAMATIZED, never re-litigates); otherwise it runs *full* discover-and-revise. **promote-revision** closes the loop: because register-pass writes only into `revisions/` and never touches `draft.md`, promote-revision is the return trip that moves the newest revision back into the live `draft.md` (carrying `source_slate` + a new `source_revision`, `status: register-revised`) — keeping `draft.md` a mirror of the newest promoted revision. **canon-sync** runs when the chapter has *landed* (post-promote): it derives project state from the landed `draft.md` — `REFERENCE/story-so-far.md` + `REFERENCE/bible.md` + `REFERENCE/threads.md` (open reader-promises: planted → advanced → paid, with blind-read pickup harvested from the run's `pass-1-blind.md` — the `^obs-011` fix) (per-fact provenance `(CH<N> rev<M>)`, idempotent per chapter) and the chapter's `continuity.md` end-state sections (the previously unowned template — `^obs-010`); additions write, contradictions gate for CRE's ruling. The forward half of `^obs-011` is the per-chapter **`brief.md`** (intent: job, beats, setups, payoffs due, seal schedule) — written before dictation, graded against after Pass 1; spec material the blind read never loads. Its outputs feed the **next** chapter's dictation-preflight, which now resolves implicit POV/place/state from REFERENCE first and back-walks prior chapters only as fallback. dictation-cleanup is the protective copy-edit (slate or any prose → polished copy-edit, **non-destructive**). They chain on projects that have adopted the per-chapter folder convention; register-pass additionally requires a project register; on projects that haven't adopted the convention, only dictation-cleanup applies. See [[_OBSERVATIONS#^obs-003]] for the rationale.

Craft content is the five-layer stack at [[KNOWLEDGE/CRAFT CANON]]. Craft observations go to [[_CREATIVE OBSERVATIONS]] under DIR-003. Installed skills inherit at their next repack (DIR-009).

`register.md` = how to revise (authority) · `voice-spec.md` = the empirical voice fingerprint the prose should match (measured target) · `contamination-checklist.md` = the named AI failure modes to scan for at the generative steps (guard). voice-spec + contamination run as soft checks in register-pass Step 2.5 and the transcoder; both flag for CRE, neither overrides the register.

## QA tiers — reader diagnostics scope to the reader unit (CRE-ruled 2026-08-03)

> A blind read is valid *because* the reader has no context. For a WIW episode that maps to reality — the episode **is** the reader unit, complete in itself. For a mid-book novel chapter it is structurally artificial: a real reader arrives carrying every prior chapter, so a per-chapter cold read over-flags planting that earlier chapters already did and cannot see the defects that only exist at book scale (motif fatigue across chapters, pacing plateaus, a stalled arc). Reader diagnostics therefore scope to the **reader unit**, and the drafting loop stays momentum-protected — the DIR-017 principle one level up. **This supersedes any per-chapter blind-read framing elsewhere in the canon** (the `chapter-pipeline` inner loop was rewired same day; the "spec-check between slate and register" framing above predates this ruling — Pass 1 now runs at tier 3/4, Passes 2–5 remain in-route line QA).

- **Tier 1 — in-route, per chapter (the drafting loop).** v6 route: spine-review gate (with the v6.1 scene-map leg) → prose-expansion → register sweep — one-sweep runner: [[WORKFLOWS/chapter-clean]] (two CRE sittings: author gate + end gate). Dictation route: cleanup → Workshop-2 warm read → loop-clearer → line battery. [[WORKFLOWS/clean-mode]] applies on trigger. **No reader-diagnostic ceremony lives in this tier.**
- **Tier 2 — pull-only, on demand.** `workshop-chapter` (a chapter in progress feels wrong), `choreographer` (an event wants dramaturgy), `scene-intensity` on one chapter, and a blind-read **canary** on a sequence's opening chapter — each on CRE's word, never scheduled, never a gate.
- **Tier 3 — part boundary (recommended checkpoint).** One blind read of the completed part: big enough to be an honest cold read, early enough that a book-scale defect is caught before more chapters compound it.
- **Tier 4 — manuscript complete (the reader battery).** CRE's own line pass first, then: blind read of the whole → `panel-read` if the heavier instrument is wanted → `scene-intensity` contour across chapters → spec passes where the reads point.
- **The episode route scopes its reads to the episode** — the unit is complete, so a per-episode cold read is honest. *(Amended 2026-09-04: v4 moved both blind reads after CRE's author pass and put `dev-edit` in the pre-author-pass slot. The reader-unit principle is unchanged; only the placement moved — see the v4 block below.)*

## The episode route (Writing Is War shorts) — v4, ruled 2026-09-04

> **v4 (2026-09-04, CRE-ruled across one session).** The route reorganizes around one idea: **CRE's author pass at S7 is the centre of gravity** — everything upstream serves it, everything downstream tests it, and no reader-diagnostic is spent on prose that is not yet his. Five changes from v3: a **dream-catching leg at S2** (`brainstorm` → `dev-capture` into the new shared `WRITING/SHORTS/DEV/` tree, short profile) so story material is caught off the drafting mic, replacing the old passive "sit"; **mandatory blueprint regeneration at S3**; **`dev-edit` as the sole pre-author-pass cold instrument at S6**; **both blind reads moved after the author pass** (S8 originates, S11 is a QA loop that closes on CRE's ruling alone); and the **EAR seat unbundled** from the S9 story panel to ride the S12 line pass on locked text. Stage numbering is preserved from v2 wherever a sibling doc keys to it — S4 is still the runway carve (`episode-runway` Pass 2), S5 still the drafting engine, and `dev-edit`'s own "S6a" position now lands exactly on S6. Provenance and the full evidence base: [[WORKFLOWS/intents/wiw-route-v4]]. **Iron rules that ride the whole route:** the AI never writes your prose (anomaly line, beats, expansion — all yours) · tier/TOS/container move by your ruling, never by edit · **no cold read before your author pass — `dev-edit`'s blind dev seats are the pre-author-pass instrument** (v4; supersedes the old "one-shot, spend it after the transcoder" rule) · the S11 loop closes on your ruling alone · `draft.md` is the sole editable copy once a slate lands · every pass reconciles against the draft, never the runway or the blueprint — **divergence is a win at the mic, not at the planning stage** (CRE-ruled 2026-09-04), so the blueprint may be tight and only the runway stays loose.

> **Two engines at S4–S5, ruled 2026-08-23.** The table above describes the **mic route** (runway → dictate → clean → transcode → slate). A second engine now exists: the **desk route** — typed, dialogue-first, edit-in-the-loop, feedback taken in real time from an intent-aware reader, narration layered last (first run: EP 02 HARD LEMONADE). The two are not variants of each other and must not be merged: the mic route is forward-only and protected from editing by **DIR-017**; the desk route makes editing the motion. **S4 is optional and belongs to the mic route only** — see `episode-runway` Pass 2. On the desk route `runway.md` and `envelope.md` are N/A-stubbed with the reason recorded, and the draft lands directly as `draft.md` with no slate. Everything from S6 on is shared. *(v4: S2's dream-catching leg is route-agnostic — it runs before either engine, and the desk route needs it as much as the mic route does.)* Canon for the desk leg: [[WORKFLOWS/desk-draft]] *(pending — spec'd from the EP 02 run)*.

### Shorts battery profile — v3 density/ordering law, RULED 2026-08-15 ([[DECISIONS/2026-08-15 wiw-shorts-battery-profile|dec-028]]); clauses 1 + 5 AMENDED 2026-09-04 (route v4)

> Graduated from the EP 01 archaeology ([[SYSTEM/reports/2026-08-15-ep01-revision-process-archaeology]]): EP 01 ran ~25 tool invocations and 4 unscheduled author rewrites; three waves of line-grade work were 47–77% discarded by the next rewrite; every structural defect surfaced first at blind-read tier. **Pilot-marked: default until EP 02–03 prove it** (dec-008 form); named fallback if the pilot fails = the ordering rules alone (dec-028 option B). The v4 table above remains the stage reference; this block is the **battery density and ordering law** laid over it. **Route v4 (2026-09-04) layers over this profile rather than replacing it** — clauses 1 and 5 are amended in place below with their basis; clauses 2, 2a, 3, 4, 6 and 7 stand unchanged, and dec-028's pilot status and 2026-10-01 review are intact.

1. ~~**One blind read, spent early** — after the transcoder slate, feeding the dev/author response.~~ **AMENDED 2026-09-04 (v4): two blind reads, both AFTER CRE's author pass.** A transcoded slate is the machine's floor rendering of his dictation, not his prose — CRE's ruling closed it: *"until I rewrite the transcoded story what is the blind read reading?"* This entry's own evidence agrees: EP 01's read on the raw dictated draft (07-22) returned mostly STT garbles, while the read that produced the dev spec (07-25) ran on **draft 2, his own rewrite**. The single-reader-blind-read class stays in the route before the author pass — `dev-edit`'s three isolated blind dev seats carry it at S6, on the instrument built for unfinished work — so the "one instance of every QA class" test still passes. Read #1 at S8 originates; read #2 at S11 is the QA loop, and **closes when CRE rules it closed — no mechanical exit criterion** (a criterion becomes either an AI judgment about his draft, breaching CDIR-004, or decoration).
2. **The cooling read is a scheduled route step, not drift** — when CRE's rewrite lands, a dated "cooling read EP NN" item (5–7 days out) goes to TASKS so day-launch serves it; no line-grade or derive work runs before it. (The unplanned version of this gap produced EP 01's draft 7 and staled the entire record-prep layer.)
2a. **The premise-alignment probe rides the cooling read** (folded in 2026-08-15, CRE-ruled, same session as the profile). Before the post-cooling rewrite, CRE answers three questions — at the mic or in the margin, two minutes: **(1)** What would you title this draft *today*? (drift from the current title = a stratum below — EP 01's own title history was the depth gauge: DOOMSCROLLER → Happening Near You → Just For You); **(2)** Which engine is the prose *actually running on*, versus the one the premise gate ruled? **(3)** Where does the draft still obey the seed's promised bloom instead of the ruled knot? **Diagnostic, never generative** — the answers aim the rewrite; no pass ever grades the draft against them (DIR-017 stands: divergence is a win). *Why it exists:* EP 01's deepest ruling — dec-017's engagement-as-cause, made 07-17, pre-prose — went unexpressed while the prose ran on the decoy engine (proximity) through draft 6; nothing in the route checked draft against ruling, so the lag was five drafts instead of one.
3. **Structure-lock gate** — register / line-edit / floor / expansion / protections / choreo wait until CRE declares the beats final, post-cooling-read. One early **mechanical-only** pass is allowed (STT garble, tense, format rulings — the useful part of EP 01's register rev 2).
4. **One combined line pass** — mechanical+line+floor as a single sheet, single CRE ruling session, after structure-lock.
5. ~~**One 4-seat panel incl. EAR, on near-final text** — the consensus + ear instrument, spent once, late.~~ **AMENDED 2026-09-04 (v4): story seats pre-lock at S9; the EAR seat unbundles to the S12 line pass.** Two corrections. **(a) "Near-final" is obsolete, not relaxed** — the clause assumed the panel was the route's last instrument; in v4 it is not (the S11 QA loop is), so the panel runs post-author-pass but **pre-structure-lock**, where a finding can still drive a rewrite. A panel run after the lock can only report findings CRE is forbidden to act on, which makes the instrument decorative. **(b) The "53% replaced the next day" figure does not apply to a panel** — it measures *line-grade polish* thrown away by a rewrite. A panel is a **diagnostic**: a finding that causes a rewrite is the instrument working. EP 03's panel produced a small revision, EP 01's a large one; both correct. CRE: *"my job isn't to hold the panel results as rulings — it identifies strengths and weaknesses from different perspectives that lead to my most ROBUST and evidence backed revision."* What genuinely perishes is the panel's **performance side** — the EAR harvest and its derives, the exact failure clause 6 governs — so EAR rides the line pass on locked text instead (*"the EAR read is meaningless to me until the revisions are done"*). Story seats: SLUSH · SUPERFAN · GENERAL · DNF, + SKEPTIC on dark content.
6. **Protections and one-way derives (performance-notes, record-script) formalize at the record gate only.** During drafting, a "don't sand" list rides draft frontmatter informally — no rulings, no witnesses to re-cite at every rewrite.
7. **Slot rule** — a flag recurring at the same structural position after a content swap escalates to "compress or restructure the slot," never another swap (EP 01's video-2 sag survived two content replacements and died to a net-zero-word compression).

Target shape: ~8 tool invocations and two **scheduled** author-generative occasions per episode. What this cuts is repeated confirmation, never coverage — one instance of every QA class that moved EP 01 is retained.

> **v4 count, 2026-09-04.** Five author-generative occasions, all scheduled: the draft (S5), the author pass (S7), the blind-read revision (S8), the panel revision (S9), and the post-cooling touch-up (S10). EP 01 ran seven, unscheduled. The v3 target of two was measured against a route with no dream-catching leg and one cold read; v4 trades two extra scheduled occasions for a draft that arrives developed and a revision loop that terminates on CRE's ruling. The clause-1 and clause-5 amendments above are the only coverage changes; every other QA class is untouched, so the "one instance of every class" test still passes.
