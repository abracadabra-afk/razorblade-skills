# premise-forge

The **premise developer** — an attended, feeling-first instrument with three jobs: (1) keep the WIW `CANDIDATES/` bag stocked with gut-ratified, short-form-contained story premises, so a weekend creative slot always presents work instead of demanding origination (the founding mission; the upstream-most tool in the episode route, formalizing S0); (2) **size any story idea CRE brings** against the format spectrum — short / novella / novel / series — honing it toward the container it actually wants (the format-aware extension, CRE-ruled 2026-08-09 same session); and (3) **deepen the one candidate CRE has picked** for a slot, at pick time, by walking the rest of the Craft Beliefs arc chain with him and recording his answers (the DEEPEN mode, v2, 2026-09-04). One tool, one measurement, different buckets.

**The problem it exists to kill:** when no work is presented, CRE spends his highest-creative-level time *creating* work rather than *executing* it — the executive-dysfunction / decision-overwhelm tax. premise-forge time-shifts premise origination into a deliberate session so the mic never waits on a blank page.

> **Design provenance (CRE-ruled 2026-08-09, premise-forge design session):** the original three-skill concept (market-trend runner → premise developer → pre-dictation scaffold) was collapsed to this one skill. The market layer was deleted on CRE's ruling — *"I won't be interested in the story at all if I'm trying to check the box for a 'market'"* — trend-chasing is a trap he named from experience. The pre-dictation scaffold already exists as the episode route (episode-init → episode-runway). What remained is this: feeling-first premise development, gut-gated, banked.
>
> **Format-aware amendment (CRE-ruled 2026-08-09, same session):** generalized from shorts-only to the format spectrum — same measurement, different buckets. Three modes (FILL / DECLARED / OPEN), format verdict in Step 6 (world-load axis added), larger formats bank to `WRITING/SEEDS/` (sized, never scheduled), the bag stays shorts-pure, and the generation carve-out stays FILL-mode-only.
>
> **v2 — DEEPEN amendment (2026-09-04, off [[WORKFLOWS/intents/premise-forge-v2]]).** The bag only half-solved its founding problem. It handed the route a knot, a scenario, a constraint and a peg — and [[WORKFLOWS/episode-blueprint]], the tool built to catch escalation variance and length before drafting, stops dead unless the escalations are articulated somewhere. Nothing upstream articulated them, so the plan session opened by making CRE originate cold: the exact tax the bag exists to remove. DEEPEN closes that seam at **pick time on the single candidate he has chosen** — it asks the rest of the arc chain and records his answers; it never proposes one. The bag itself is deliberately left shallow (see the DEEPEN section's cost note).

## Modes (declared at session open)

In the three forge modes the tool **always measures**, and the mode only sets the target format and what happens on a mismatch. **DEEPEN is not a measuring mode** — it is the pick-time capture pass, and it is the one row below that runs no verdict at all:

| Mode | CRE says | Target | On a larger/mismatched verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FILL** | "fill the bag" / "forge the premises" / "I need shorts" | short-form, locked | **Size, bank to SEEDS, park — never develop in-session.** The session returns to shorts. |
| **DECLARED** | "here's an idea for a series/novel/novella" | CRE's declaration | Honing shapes toward the target; if the measurement contradicts it ("you said novella; this measures like a series"), surface with a one-line basis — **CRE rules.** A genuine format fork (novella vs. duology) hands to decision-helper. |
| **OPEN** | "here's an idea — I don't know what it wants to be" | none | The format verdict converges *through* the honing conversation; it lands when the premise stops moving. |
| **DEEPEN** (v2) | "deepen the premise" / "run the deepening" / "prep for blueprint" | the one banked short-form candidate CRE has **picked for a slot** | Not a format mode — it measures nothing. It walks the rest of the arc chain with him and records his answers into that candidate's `triage.md`. **Enters at the DEEPEN section, not at Step 2**; Steps 2–8 do not run. |

**The mode boundary is a guard, not a convenience.** In FILL mode a shiny larger idea is the executive-dysfunction failure wearing a productivity costume — the known gravity is that everything blows into a novel, and a Saturday fill session must not wander off following one. Two minutes to size and bank it; then back to shorts. No trigger stated → default by opener: "fill the bag"-family → FILL; "here's an idea…" → DECLARED or OPEN per whether he names a format; "deepen"/"prep for blueprint" + a named candidate → DEEPEN.

**DEEPEN's boundary is the same guard pointed the other way.** FILL protects the session from developing the wrong thing; DEEPEN protects the *bag* from being developed at all. It runs on one picked candidate, after the pick, and never in bulk — the reasons are in the DEEPEN section.

## The sanctioned-generation boundary

Every gate in this vault carries the organic-process guard: *never invents story options.* premise-forge is the one deliberate exception, and the carve-out is narrow:

- **Generation is sanctioned at the what-if layer only, and only in FILL mode**, per CRE's own standing instruction in [[WRITING/SHORTS/PREMISES]] — a document that literally instructs an AI to produce *short-form* premises in his format. This workflow formalizes that existing, CRE-ruled practice. **In DECLARED and OPEN modes the tool generates nothing**: CRE brings the idea; the tool sizes, challenges, and hones his material — cleanly inside the guard, no carve-out needed. "Brainstorm me novel premises" would be a new ruling, made explicitly, never inherited from this one.
- **The carve-out stops at the premise sentence. It does not reach the arc chain** (v2, 2026-09-04). DEEPEN generates nothing in any mode — not a choice, not an escalation, not an angle, not a failure mode, not a mirror, not a moment of truth, not an ending. Those are story options and CDIR-003 forbids inventing them. The carve-out was ruled for the what-if layer in a document that asks for what-ifs; widening it downward to the chain would be **a new ruling, made explicitly, never inherited** — the same sentence that governs "brainstorm me novel premises," applied to the same boundary from the other side. A DEEPEN field CRE has not answered is marked `[NOT NAMED — CRE]` and left.
- **Authorship lives in CRE's selection and honing.** Nothing enters the bag without his felt yes; his language always supersedes proposed phrasing.
- **Everything downstream stays guarded.** Beats, scenes, dialogue, prose — the guard holds in full. This skill never writes a word of story.

## Pipeline position

```
premise-forge (S0)    →  bag: CANDIDATES/<title>/triage.md
                            ↓  CRE picks (weekend slot / next-up)
premise-forge (S0.5)  →  DEEPEN — arc chain into that candidate's triage.md   ← v2, this mode
                            ↓  derived handoff block names the next action
episode-init (S1)     →  gate confirms triage pre-answers → scaffold
episode-blueprint (S1.5) → the plan: escalations, angles, budgets, variance
episode-runway        →  runway carve (S4, optional — mic route)
mic → drafting engine → rough draft banked
                            ↓  THE SIT (between rough draft and author pass — CRE-ruled 2026-08-09)
dev-edit → AUTHOR PASS → panel-read → line passes → record prep
```

**Why S0.5 sits before the gate, not after.** The deepening is felt material, and the felt material is hottest at the moment he picks. Putting it after `episode-init` would mean gating a premise, scaffolding a folder, and only then discovering the chain does not hold. It is also cheap here: nothing has been built yet. **DEEPEN is optional** — an undeepened candidate may still be gated and blueprinted; `episode-blueprint`'s hand-back grammar still works, it just works harder. A mandatory deepening would be a gate in front of the mic, which is the one thing this route does not add.

**The sit ruling.** The sit-with-it phase lives **between rough draft and author pass**, not between gate and dictation. Bag → gate → runway → mic *same day* is sanctioned. A steady cadence of banked rough drafts means the author pass always has aged material waiting — the sit happens on drafts, not on premises.

**The two-buffer economy.** This workflow maintains buffer 1 (premise bag → feeds the mic); the drafting engine maintains buffer 2 (rough-draft bank → feeds the author pass). A weekend slot draws from either by energy: generative → pick and dictate; editorial → author-pass a banked draft. Both refill from normal operation; work is always presented, never originated cold.

## When to use

CRE says "forge the premises" / "fill the bag" (FILL mode), brings a story idea with a named format ("here's a series idea" — DECLARED), brings an idea he can't size ("I don't know what it wants to be" — OPEN), or names a picked candidate and says "deepen the premise" / "run the deepening" / "prep for blueprint" (DEEPEN). Also fires when the bag hits its min-fill floor.

**Attended, never scheduled, in every mode** — the gut gate is the load-bearing mechanism of the first three modes and the deepening is a second judgment surface in the fourth; both would be deferred by an unattended run, which strips the whole point (DIR-012 clause 1). There is no unattended mode.

Do NOT use it to gate/scaffold an episode (episode-init), write `blueprint.md` or judge escalation variance (episode-blueprint), carve the runway or check a draft (episode-runway), synthesize a dictated talk-through (brainstorm), route dev talk (dev-capture), or **develop** larger-format material — this tool sizes and banks seeds; developing one is dev-layer work behind a portfolio decision.

## Inputs

- **A feeling / human condition** — from CRE ("loneliness," "jealousy," "self-worth," "resignation"), OR offered by the variety scan (Step 3) when he arrives without one. CRE may also point at a [[VIBES/_VIBES|VIBES]] capture fragment as the seed — *at his pointer only; never mine VIBES (or any LIFE surface — DIR-015) automatically.*
- **The premise grammar** — [[WRITING/SHORTS/PREMISES]]: knot · scenario-that-challenges-the-knot · tensional constraint · topical peg, in the one-sentence "What if…" form.
- **The corpus for variety** — `EPISODES/*/premise.md` + `CANDIDATES/*/triage.md` (knots, conditions, subgenres, constraint shapes already used).
- **Container bands** — [[BUSINESS/SUBSTACK/WRITINGISWAR - YOUTUBE CHANNEL STRATEGY]] §3b: standard ~2,000–2,800 words ≈ 15–20 min.
- **The craft, by path at run time** (DEEPEN mode; CDIR-002 — never restated inside this tool, because a copy drifts and [[KNOWLEDGE/CRAFT CANON]] says cite the layer): [[KNOWLEDGE/PROCESS/CRAFT BELIEFS]] — **"Character Arcs"** (the chain, and the rule that each escalation attacks the flaw from a different angle), **"Structure"** (failure over success), **"Conflict"** (the villain or structure as the mirror of the flaw), **"Endings"** (grey, equivalent exchange, outcomes optional, the payoff ledger). Optional where CRE reaches for curve vocabulary: [[KNOWLEDGE/REFERENCES/Methods/Tension and Transformation Framework]] — read to understand the word he used, never to assign a curve he did not state.

## Outputs

- Banked short-form candidates: `WRITING/SHORTS/CANDIDATES/<WORKING TITLE>/triage.md`, each pre-answering the episode-init Pass-1 gate.
- Banked larger-format seeds: `WRITING/SEEDS/<WORKING TITLE>/triage.md` with `format:` + the measurement that produced the verdict — sized, never scheduled.
- A ranked **next-up** (CRE-ratified, FILL mode) so the next creative slot presents one story, not a menu.
- **DEEPEN:** the picked candidate's `triage.md`, extended in place with an `## Arc chain` section in CRE's words and a derived handoff block at the foot naming every decision so far and one next action. No new file, no new folder — the bag stays one file per candidate.

## Steps

> **Mode routing:** **Step 0 runs in every mode, always.** FILL then runs all steps. DECLARED and OPEN skip the FILL machinery — no bag census, no batch generation, no gut gate on admission (CRE's own idea needs no admission) — entering at **Step 6** (measure) and iterating with **Step 7** (hone + bank); Step 8 runs only if the session also touched the bag. **DEEPEN skips Steps 1–8 entirely** and runs the [DEEPEN mode](#deepen-mode-s05--pick-time) section below; it measures nothing and banks nothing new.
>
> Step numbers 2–8 are cited by downstream docs and are deliberately not renumbered by v2. The creative-lane load is Step 0; the mode declaration that used to float in the Modes table is Step 1.

### Step 0 — Sentinel, then the creative-lane load
1. **Sentinel** (`^obs-004`): verify `_DIRECTIVES.md` frontmatter (`type: ai-os-brain`, `file: directives`). Fail → **halt** and ask which folder is the vault.
2. **Creative-lane load:** read `_CREATIVE DIRECTIVES.md` (CDIR-001–010) **after `_DIRECTIVES` and before opening any `WRITING/` file or craft doc.** This is a Lane 5 + Lane 1 tool and the load rule is ratified (DIR-002, CRE 2026-09-03); the v1 doc predated the ratification and had no load step. `_DIRECTIVES` wins on OS matters, `_CREATIVE DIRECTIVES` on craft-behavior, CRE's instinct over both.
3. **Attended check:** not attended → stop and say why (DIR-012). Both the gut gate and the deepening are judgment surfaces.

### Step 1 — Declare the mode
FILL / DECLARED / OPEN / DEEPEN, per the Modes table. Stated trigger wins; otherwise infer from the opener and **say which mode you took in one line** before proceeding, so a wrong read costs a sentence rather than a session.

### Step 2 — Bag census
Read `CANDIDATES/` (a `Glob` miss is never evidence of absence — confirm any negative with a direct read, DIR-005). Report: live candidates, their conditions/knots, ages, and any **stale flags** (Step 8). If the bag is at cap (~10–12 live), lead with hygiene (Step 8) before forging more — an overfull bag reintroduces the decision overwhelm this exists to kill.

### Step 3 — Condition intake + variety scan
Two entries:

- **CRE brings the condition** → run the variety scan as a freshness check only ("you've worked isolation twice — still game, or angle it differently?"). His condition wins regardless; the scan informs, never vetoes.
- **CRE arrives open** → run the scan as the offer: conditions/knot-families already worked vs. untouched ("worked: isolation ×2, dread ×3; untouched: envy, self-worth, grief-adjacent shame"). Offer 2–3 untouched or under-worked conditions. **He picks; the scan never picks for him.**

The scan reads the corpus (Inputs) — never the market. Variety comes from CRE's own body of work.

**Call `interview-me`, don't improvise the questions** (v2). Where this step needs more than the condition — which of two conditions, whether a worked knot is still live, what the peg's moment actually is — hand the gap list to [[WORKFLOWS/interview-me]] as a caller frame rather than asking one question at a time. It batches 5–7 with defaults so one reply ratifies the lot, and it never asks what the tree already answers (DIR-011). Story-content questions carry **no default** — that is interview-me's own rule and it is what keeps the call inside CDIR-003.

### Step 4 — Forge (the sanctioned what-if layer)
Generate what-if premises around the chosen condition, **batch of ~5–10, one condition per batch**, each in the full PREMISES grammar:

1. **Knot first, always.** Start from the character flaw/condition ("paranoid," "isolated and resigned"). The knot is the premise's spine — never reverse-engineer a knot onto a scenario.
2. **Scenario that directly challenges the knot.** The situation must detonate the flaw, not merely coexist with it.
3. **Tensional constraint.** A clock, a countdown, a closing door — and prefer constraints that do *containment* work (a 20-minute clock is also a container; see Step 6).
4. **Peg from the condition, never from a market.** The peg is the contemporary costume the timeless condition wears — ask *"what does this condition look like right now?"* (isolation → doomscrolling; hypervigilance → baby monitors; parasocial hunger → the feed). Worked example: HAPPENING NEAR YOU — condition: isolation/resignation, *"easier to stay inside"*; peg: algorithms as substitute for touching grass. The condition produced the peg. A premise whose peg has no condition under it is a box-check, not a story.

Present the batch flat — no advocacy, no ranking at this stage.

### Step 5 — Gut gate (CRE rules every candidate)
CRE's felt response is the only gate. Per candidate:

- **SPARK** — the gut kicked in → proceeds to Step 6.
- **REWORK** — something's there but the angle is off → CRE names what; re-forge that one.
- **KILL** — discard freely, zero sunk cost, no defense of the corpse. Expect most of a batch to die; that is the instrument working, not failing.

**Never argue for a killed premise. Never smooth a broken one into shape uninvited** — name what's missing and hand it back (the episode-init gate's own rule). A bag of market-optimal premises CRE is lukewarm on is worse than an empty bag: he won't dictate them, and the bag teaches him to distrust it.

On **REWORK**, route the "what's off" through `interview-me` (v2) rather than a volley of single questions — one batch, no defaults on the story-content items, and the re-forge runs off his answer.

### Step 6 — Scope measurement (the format verdict)
CRE's named weakness runs opposite to most: an under-contained story **blows up into a novel**, and paring down costs him more than expanding. Catch the sizing at conception — the cheapest point. Each SPARK (FILL mode) or brought idea (DECLARED/OPEN) is measured on the containment dimensions:

- **Knots** — one flaw under pressure, or a knot ensemble?
- **Escalation spines** — one line of pressure rising to one detonation, or braided/sequential spines?
- **The constraint's temporal span** — a clock that bounds the story's world (20 minutes, one blackout, one battery bar) vs. one that stretches ("over the following weeks…" is a novel leak; generational span is series gravity).
- **Cast** — protagonist + ≤2 load-bearing others, or an ensemble?
- **World-load** — does the premise demand a world with its own rules, economies, and history (Godsrift-shaped), or does it run on the reader's own world plus one intrusion (WIW-shaped)? High world-load is the strongest series signal.
- **Container bands** — natural telling in ~2,000–2,800 words? (Two-band gate, strategy §3b.)

**Verdict per premise — the format spectrum:** **SHORT-FORM · NOVELLA · NOVEL · SERIES**, recommendation with a one-line basis, measured dimensions named. **CRE rules.** None of these verdicts is a failure. Then by mode:

- **FILL:** SHORT-FORM proceeds to Step 7 (bag). Anything larger → sized and banked to SEEDS (Step 7), parked, session returns to shorts.
- **DECLARED:** verdict matches the declaration → hone toward it. Contradiction → surface it; CRE rules the format (a real fork hands to decision-helper). Then Step 7 banks per the ruled format.
- **OPEN:** the verdict converges through honing; when the premise stops moving, it lands, and Step 7 banks per the landed format.

### Step 7 — Hone + bank (two destinations, by format)
Conversationally sharpen each survivor with CRE — tighten the what-if sentence, sharpen the constraint, confirm the peg. **His phrasing always supersedes proposed phrasing.** Then bank by format verdict:

**SHORT-FORM → the bag:** `WRITING/SHORTS/CANDIDATES/<WORKING TITLE>/triage.md`. The bag stays **shorts-pure, always** — that purity is what makes the weekend slot decision-free.

**NOVELLA / NOVEL / SERIES → the seedbed:** `WRITING/SEEDS/<WORKING TITLE>/triage.md`, same triage convention plus a `format:` frontmatter field and a `## Format measurement` section recording the dimensions that produced the verdict. **A seed is sized, not scheduled:** the seedbed is a parking lot with good labels, never a queue. A seed entering actual development is a **portfolio-residency decision** — decision-helper + CRE, outside this tool entirely (week-shape's guard: story residency is never reordered by an instrument).

Both destinations: working title from CRE (never invent one he hasn't at least shrugged at), frontmatter serialized per DIR-004, following the existing candidate-triage convention:

```
---
type: candidate-triage
working_title: <TITLE>
author: Chad Ryan
source: premise-forge session YYYY-MM-DD
condition: <the feeling/human condition>
format: short-form   # seeds carry novella | novel | series
triaged: YYYY-MM-DD
status: banked premise — awaiting CRE pick + episode-init gate   # seeds: "seed — sized, not scheduled; development is a portfolio decision"
---
# <TITLE> — forged premise
## The what-if        (the honed one-sentence premise, verbatim as CRE ratified it)
## Character knot     (the flaw + the condition under it)
## Tensional constraint (and the containment work it does)
## Peg                (the contemporary costume — and its condition source)
## Container          (band verdict + why it holds short)
## TOS band / tier    (best pre-read: free-tier / age-restricted / never → FULL / SAFE-CUT / TEASE rec — episode-init re-rules at gate)
## Variety note       (what this adds against the worked corpus)
## Format measurement (the Step-6 verdict + the dimensions that produced it; shorts: the containing elements named)

## Arc chain          (DEEPEN only — written at pick time, never at forge time; absent until then)
## Handoff            (DEEPEN only — derived from the sections above; regenerate, never hand-edit)
```

Steps 1–8 write everything down to `## Format measurement` and stop there. **The last two sections are the DEEPEN mode's and appear only on a picked candidate** — their absence on a banked candidate is the normal state, not a gap.

Where the fields above are thin, batch the remainder through `interview-me` (v2) rather than asking them one at a time; his phrasing supersedes any proposed phrasing, here as everywhere.

The triage pre-answers the episode-init Pass-1 gate. **It does not bind that gate** (amended 2026-09-04): init reconciles the triage against the live material first, and where they disagree the material wins — a triage is a dated claim and stories move between banking and gating. Write it as the best account at forge time, not as a contract. Candidates stay **unnumbered** — episode numbering is CRE's scheduling call, always (episode-init's rule).

### Step 8 — Bag hygiene + next-up
- **Cap:** ~10–12 live candidates. At cap, forge nothing new until CRE retires or promotes.
- **Min-fill floor:** below **4** live candidates → surface "fill the bag" as a task to `TASKS/TASKS.md` so it rides week-shape/day-launch onto the board (DIR-012 clause 5's logic: a floor nobody serves is a dead letter).
- **Staleness:** a candidate's **peg is a dated claim** (DIR-010). At ~90 days, or when a peg's moment has visibly passed, flag for **re-gut**: CRE re-rules SPARK (peg refresh if needed) or KILL. Never auto-retire — the checker flags; CRE rules (DIR-014's matcher lesson: the distinguishing fact isn't in the text).
- **Next-up:** close every session by proposing a ranked next-up from the live bag (recency of gut-yes, peg freshness, variety vs. the last published episode). **CRE ratifies or reorders.** The weekend slot then presents one story, zero decisions.

## DEEPEN mode (S0.5 — pick time)

**Fires when** CRE names a banked candidate and says "deepen the premise," "run the deepening," or "prep for blueprint" — i.e. he has picked the story for a slot and wants the arc chain decided before the plan session opens.

**What it does:** asks him the rest of the Craft Beliefs arc chain, records his answers verbatim into that candidate's `triage.md`, and derives a handoff block naming every decision so far and one next action. **It asks; it never proposes.** No measurement, no verdict, no ranking, no new candidate.

**Why one candidate, at pick time, and never the bag.** A bag of 10–12 each carrying a full arc chain pays development cost on candidates that die on the shelf or go stale at the 90-day peg re-gut — and an over-worked bag reintroduces the decision overwhelm this tool exists to kill (Step 8's cap is the same logic). The bag is deliberately shallow. Depth is bought once, on the one story that is actually going to be told, at the moment the felt material is hottest.

**It refuses, out loud, and stops:**

- **The bag in bulk** ("deepen all of them," "deepen the top three"). Name the reason above; offer to deepen the one he picks.
- **A `WRITING/SEEDS/` entry.** Seeds are sized, not scheduled; a seed entering development is a portfolio-residency decision (decision-helper + CRE), not this mode's move.
- **An unpicked candidate**, unless he says he is picking it now — in which case the pick *is* the answer and the mode proceeds.
- **An unattended run.** The deepening is a judgment surface (DIR-012).

### D1 — Locate the candidate and read what exists

`WRITING/SHORTS/CANDIDATES/<WORKING TITLE>/triage.md`. Named without a path → search there; several matches → ask, never guess. A `Glob` miss is not evidence of absence — confirm with a direct read (DIR-005).

Then check whether the episode exists yet: an `EP NN - *` folder under `WRITING/SHORTS/EPISODES/` whose `premise.md` names this candidate, or a `status: promoted → EP NN` stamp on the triage. **Gated (folder + `premise.md` present) → the next action is `episode-blueprint`. Not gated → `episode-init`.** That single fact is what D6's block turns on; establish it before the interview, not after.

Also read: `DECISIONS/_QUICK LOG.md` rows naming this candidate — prior rulings are not re-asked (CDIR-009).

### D2 — Read the craft by path

`KNOWLEDGE/PROCESS/CRAFT BELIEFS.md` — "Character Arcs" (the chain and the different-angle rule), "Structure" (failure is where the story thrives), "Conflict" (the villain, structure or system as the mirror), "Endings" (grey, equivalent exchange, outcomes optional, the payoff ledger and the scam test). Read them fresh, every run. **Never restate them inside this tool or paraphrase them into a system of its own** — they are CRE's ruled craft and a copy drifts (CDIR-002; [[KNOWLEDGE/CRAFT CANON]] says cite the layer). They set the *questions*; they never supply the answers.

The chain is `Flaw → Incident → Choice → Escalations → Failures → Moment of Truth → Outcome`. The triage already carries **Flaw** (the knot) and **Incident** (the scenario that challenges it), written when the candidate was banked. **Failures are not a separate field** — the beliefs define them as what each escalation produces, so each escalation line carries its own failure mode. This mode covers the rest.

**Two sections, not one.** The chain's terminal term is a binary — *does the character transform, or do they fail?* — and the field the route needs is the **stance**: what is gained, what is lost, and whether the outcome is stated or inferred. That material is in **"Endings,"** not in the chain. Read both, or the most consequential question in the piece gets asked as a yes/no (`^cobs-007`).

### D3 — Extract before you ask (DIR-011)

Fill everything the triage, `PREMISES.md`, and the `DECISIONS/` rows already answer, quoting him. A question the tree answers is never asked; it presents as *"resolved against the triage — confirm"* (one tap). Only tree-silent fields go to the interview.

### D4 — The interview

Hand the remaining fields to [[WORKFLOWS/interview-me]] as the caller frame. Never reimplement it: it batches 5–7 questions per round with max 3 rounds, built for the ADHD profile so one reply ratifies the lot.

**Every field below is story content, so every question carries no default** — that is interview-me's own rule ("if a question would need you to propose a story beat, a premise, or an option he has not articulated, you do not propose it; ask the question and leave the default blank"), and it is exactly what keeps this call inside CDIR-003. A suggested escalation is not a helpful default; it is the tool writing his story.

The frame:

| Field | The question underneath | Belief it serves |
|---|---|---|
| **Choice** | What decision or action commits this character to the journey? | Character Arcs |
| **Escalations** | What challenges come, in order, each worse than the last? | Character Arcs |
| **— angle** (per escalation) | How does *this one* attack the flaw differently from the others? | Character Arcs (the different-angle rule) |
| **— failure mode** (per escalation) | Does he fail forward here, or take a false victory that entrenches the flaw? | Structure · Character Arcs |
| **Mirror** | Who or what embodies the flaw and works to stop the change — a person, a structure, a system? | Conflict |
| **Moment of Truth** | At the end of the staircase, does he change or reinforce? | Character Arcs |
| **Ending stance** | What is gained, what is lost — and is the outcome stated or left for the reader? | Endings |
| **Curve** | *Only if he reaches for one.* Never asked cold, never inferred. | T&T vocabulary |

Stop when he says enough. Unanswered fields are not a failure state — they are the honest record, and `episode-blueprint` is built to hand them back.

**DIR-015 holds.** If the session opens a vein about CRE's own state rather than the story's, name the affective lane once, plainly, and never work it. Take what he has given, write it, end.

### D5 — Write the `## Arc chain` section

Extend `triage.md` in place with the file tools (DIR-005). Place `## Arc chain` after `## Format measurement`. His words, verbatim — no upgrading his phrasing into craft jargon, no smoothing.

```

> `[NOT NAMED — CRE]` = asked and not answered. A gap, never a fill.

**Choice** — his sentence
**Mirror** — his phrase · type: person | structure | system
**Escalations**
- **E1** — what it does to the knot · angle: his phrase · fails as: false victory | fail forward
- **E2** — his sentence · angle: [NOT NAMED — CRE] · fails as: fail forward
- **E3** — [NOT NAMED — CRE]
**Moment of Truth** — his sentence
**Ending stance** — gained: … · lost: … · outcome: stated | inferred
**Curve** — his stated curve, verbatim | [NOT NAMED — CRE]
```

**One line or one short paragraph per field.** The register is the existing triage's: headed, flat, declarative. **Nothing here may be readable aloud as story** — no quoted speech, no rendered image, no clock time, no object, no name he has not given. A line names *what a beat does to the flaw*, never *what happens on the page*; the test is whether you would be embarrassed to find the sentence in his draft. If it could survive verbatim into the story, it does not belong here (CDIR-001 — material written here pre-spends the mic).

**What this section does not do:** it does not judge whether two angles are really the same angle, or whether the shape repeats recent episodes. Capture only. That read is `episode-blueprint`'s variance line, and two tools measuring one field will disagree — the same reason corpus variety of condition/knot/genre was ruled to stay here and out of the blueprint (CRE, 2026-09-03). It also does not re-measure scope, container or format: **Step 6 is untouched**, and the blueprint's scope line already reuses those dimensions post-gate.

Frontmatter: add `deepened: YYYY-MM-DD` and `next_action: episode-init | episode-blueprint`, serialized (`yaml.safe_dump`) and parse-gated (DIR-004). Never hand-format a new key with an unquoted colon or `#`.

## Stop conditions

- Sentinel fails → halt.
- Not attended, any mode → stop and say why (DIR-012).
- CRE's gut isn't firing on anything in a batch → **stop forging, don't brute-force volume.** Offer a different condition or end the session; a forced bag is a distrusted bag.
- Any pull toward drafting beats, scenes, or prose → out of scope; that's the mic's job (DIR-017 protects the forward flow) and the route's.
- **DEEPEN:** asked to deepen the bag in bulk, a `SEEDS/` entry, or a candidate CRE has not picked → refuse with the reason and offer the one-candidate run. No `triage.md` at the named path → stop; this mode extends an existing candidate, it never creates one.
- **DEEPEN:** CRE answers nothing at all → write nothing. A file of `[NOT NAMED — CRE]` is noise, and the mode has no other content to add.

## Guards (summary)

- **Attended, never scheduled — every mode.** The gut gate is the mechanism of the first three; the deepening is a second judgment surface in the fourth (DIR-012).
- **Creative-lane load every run** — `_CREATIVE DIRECTIVES` after `_DIRECTIVES`, before any `WRITING/` file (DIR-002, Step 0).
- **Generation sanctioned at the what-if layer only, FILL mode only** (PREMISES.md carve-out); DECLARED/OPEN work CRE's own material; never a word of story prose, beats, or scene work in any mode.
- **The carve-out does not reach the arc chain.** DEEPEN asks and records; it never proposes a choice, escalation, angle, failure mode, mirror, moment of truth, ending, or curve (CDIR-003). Unanswered is `[NOT NAMED — CRE]`.
- **Deepen one picked candidate, at pick time — never the bag, never a seed.** Depth is bought once, on the story that is going to be told.
- **Capture escalation angles; never judge their variance.** That read is `episode-blueprint`'s; two tools on one field will disagree.
- **Never re-measure scope, container, or format in DEEPEN.** Step 6 owns those and the blueprint's scope line reuses them post-gate.
- **Craft read by path every run; never restated inside the tool** (CDIR-002; CRAFT CANON says cite the layer).
- **The handoff block is derived and regenerated, never hand-edited** (DIR-019 §1) — a hand-edited mirror is a second disagreeing record.
- **Knot-first, peg-from-condition, never market-first.** No trend research, no market scans — CRE ruled the market layer out entirely.
- **CRE's felt yes is the only admission to the bag; kills are free.**
- **The mode boundary holds in FILL mode:** larger ideas are sized and banked in minutes, never developed — the session returns to shorts.
- **The bag stays shorts-pure; larger formats bank to `WRITING/SEEDS/` — sized, never scheduled.** A seed entering development is a portfolio-residency decision (decision-helper + CRE), never this tool's move.
- **Episode numbers are CRE's; working titles are CRE's; his phrasing supersedes proposed phrasing** — in DEEPEN as everywhere. The deepening is not a back door around rules that already govern the bag.
- **Serialized, parse-gated frontmatter; file-tool writes; re-read after** (DIR-004, DIR-005).
- **DIR-015:** if a session opens a vein about CRE's own state rather than a story condition, the affective lane is named, never worked.

## What this does NOT fix

The bag guarantees **presented work, not flow**. A stall in front of a stocked bag is `work-through` territory (executional lane, DIR-015 governs), not a bag defect — the bag can't help and shouldn't try.

DEEPEN guarantees **decided material, not a good story**. It records the chain CRE named; whether an angle is really distinct, whether the ending stance is the right one, and whether the shape has been used lately are craft judgments — his at the mic, the blueprint's at the plan. This mode is deliberately not the judge of any of them (DIR-018: the check names its own blind spot).
