# Interlocking Arc Model

Fixed 35-beat spines plus a configuration layer. Supporting archetypes attach to landmark beats.

## Beat Chain

Each beat runs in this order: the lead enters carrying internal N-1, antagonist N acts on that state, external N happens, and the lead responds with internal N. Internal N is the escalated state carried into N+1.

- **C1 Lead-driven beats.** At 25, 31 and 32 the lead acts first and the antagonist answers.
- **C2 Setup beats (1–5).** The antagonist may be offstage. The chain locks in at 6.

## Lead Arc Rules

- **G1 Coping gradient.** Hardened (16), Entrenched (21), Brittle (26). The character grips the same coping harder each round and never changes course.
- **Recommitment beats.** 15, 20 and 25 repeat by design. They show the fall back to what is known.
- **T1 Beat 23.** The escalation hands the character a win that hides the flaw or a loss that confirms it. Either result sends them back to the flawed path.
- **T2 Beat 25.** The character stops reacting and pursues the Want head-on. This is the midpoint turn, and it is still the fallback.

## External Plot Rules

- **S1 Sync.** External N triggers or tests internal N.
- **S2 Offers (17, 22, 27).** Each needs a concrete external offer to be ignored, dismissed or refused.
- **S3 Recommitment (15, 20, 25).** Each needs an external reward or excuse that makes falling back look reasonable.
- **S4 Inner Cave (30).** The external beat supplies the information. The revelation stays internal.
- **S5 Bookend (35).** The final image mirrors beat 1.

## Antagonist Rules

- **P1 Sync.** Antagonist N drives external N and puts pressure on internal N.
- **P2 Mirror.** The antagonist is the lead's feared self: the same Want pursued through the flaw with no restraint. It can be evil or only a force of resistance. If it has no intent, read 8, 9 and 16 as "its path crosses, hardens and adapts." It needs a face or an agent at 12 and 32.
- **P3 Wants the lead unchanged.** At 14, 15, 20 and 25 it permits or rewards the fallback.
- **P4 Recognition gradient.** Mirror glimpsed at 24, seen fully at 30.
- **P5 Does not change.** Its end at 35 shows what the lead becomes by failing at 34. Redemption variant: "Antagonist Changes Because the Lead Did."
- **P6 Beat 27.** The ally's last offer and the antagonist's "Become Me" offer land together and pull in opposite directions.

## Failed Mains (Ensemble)

| Code | Exit | Exit type | Failed lead's beats compressed | Lands on external | Lead reflects at | Lesson the lead denies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | 18 | Quits | 11–15 run full, 16–29 fit into 16–17, 30–35 fit into 18 | 19 | 19, denied at 20 | The flaw costs you |
| X2 | 23 | Broken or killed | 16–20 run full, 21–29 fit into 21–22, 30–35 fit into 23 | 24 | 24, denied at 25 | The flaw costs the people around you |
| X3 | 27 | Defects | 21–25 run full, 26–29 fit into 26, 30–35 fit into 27 | 27, then a proxy at 31–32 | 27, denied at 27 | Embracing the flaw turns you into the antagonist |
| X4 | 28 | Dies | 26–29 fit into 26–27, 30–35 fit into 28 | 29 | 29 | The flaw costs everything |

- **E1 Compressed arc.** The exiting lead faces their Moment of Truth on the exit beat and fails it.
- **E2 Exit slots.** External 24, 26 and 29 show one lead's failure as another's cost.
- **E3 Defection.** Accepting "Become Me" at 27 makes the character a proxy.
- **E4 Warnings.** Each exit shows what failure at 34 looks like. Exits get worse in order.
- **E5 Timing.** An exit at 13 works only if the reader already knows that lead's flaw.
- **L1 Harvest.** The revelation at 30 is built from the denied lessons of X1–X4.
- **L2 Miniature 34s.** The lead watches the losing answer before giving the winning one.
- **L3 Fill only the slots the cast needs.**
- **L4 Order.** Quit, broken, defect, dead.

## Deuteragonist

A copy of the lead spine with its own Want, flaw and Need, set by K2 and K3.

- **D1** Shares the antagonist, which must embody the deuteragonist's fear as well, or attack the lead's flaw from a second angle. Otherwise the deuteragonist gets a proxy antagonist.

**Beat 34 ownership**

- **V1 Lead-owned.** The deuteragonist's Moment of Truth lands at 33 as the Twist.
- **V2 Reinforcement.** At 34a the lead chooses the Need and falls short externally. At 34b the deuteragonist faces their own Moment of Truth and completes the action. The lead's change must be what brings the deuteragonist in.
- **V3 Handoff.** The lead fails internally at 34a. The deuteragonist carries the climax and the lead resolves unchanged.

**Phase patterns**

- **PH1 Parallel.** Same beats, different flaw. The arcs converge at 32–34.
- **PH2 Counterpoint.** Starts near the lead's Need and slides toward the lead's flaw.
- **PH3 Staggered.** Leading (one beat ahead, Moment of Truth at 33) or trailing (one beat behind, Moment of Truth at 34b).

| | V1 | V2 | V3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PH1 Parallel | weak | yes | yes |
| PH2 Counterpoint | yes | only with redemption at 34b | no |
| PH3 Staggered | leading | trailing | trailing |

## Configuration Switches

| Code | Switch | Options |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | Lead outcome | Changed / Not changed |
| K2 | Beat 34 ownership | V1 / V2 / V3 |
| K3 | Deuteragonist phase | None / PH1 / PH2 / PH3 leading / PH3 trailing |
| K4 | Antagonist nature | Evil / Force of resistance, with or without intent |
| K5 | Antagonist end | Unchanged / Redeemed |
| K6 | Midpoint result | False victory / False defeat |
| K7 | Ensemble exits | Any of X1–X4, or none |
| K8 | Supporting arcs | Any of AM1–AM5, or none |

- **K-R1 Locked combinations.** K1 "Not changed" forces K2 to V3, or to a tragic ending with no deuteragonist. K2 limits K3 through the compatibility table.

- **AM7 Functional roles (no arc).** Herald at 6, threshold guardian at 10, proxies at 12 and 32, antagonist agents at 16–18. A trickster or comic foil deflates 15, 20 and 25.

## Collision Rules

- **CR1 Beat 33.** One owner: deuteragonist under V1, shapeshifter (AM5), or the antagonist's hidden layer.
- **CR2 Beat 27.** One owner: X3 or AM4.
- **CR3 Beat 29.** One owner: mentor (AM1) or X4.
- **CR4 Offers 17, 22, 27.** One owner each: mentor by default, or shapeshifter (AM5). With neither set in K8, an ally from the external spine takes them.
